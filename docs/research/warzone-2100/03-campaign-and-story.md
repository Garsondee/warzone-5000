# Campaign and Story

## Premise
Earth has been devastated by a nuclear collapse. Most survivors have become scavenger bands. The player commands
**The Project**, an organised group rebuilding civilisation using pre-war technology, which sends three teams
(**Alpha, Beta, Gamma**) to recover that technology.

## Story arc
1. **Alpha (Arizona).** You lead Team Alpha, fighting scavengers and securing artifacts. You repel the
   **New Paradigm**, a survivalist group more advanced than your forces.
2. **Beta (Chicago).** Reassigned to Team Beta, under attack by **The Collective**, a militaristic industrial faction
   in urban ruins.
3. **The twist.** A self-aware computer virus/AI, **Nexus**, is actually controlling both the New Paradigm and the
   Collective. Nexus then launches nuclear missiles at the Alpha and Beta bases, forcing a retreat to Gamma.
4. **Gamma.** Gamma's forces are already taken over by Nexus, so you are ambushed on arrival. You develop
   countermeasures to the Nexus infection; Nexus seizes orbital laser satellites. The Project captures a NASDA
   missile site and shoots the satellites down, then survivors from Alpha and Beta join a final assault that destroys Nexus.

### Origin of Nexus (sources differ)
- Wikipedia: a scientist, Dr. Reed, bankrupted by the US military, transformed himself into Nexus.
- Wikizone fan wiki: Dr. Alan Reed, CEO of the Reed Corporation, created the Nexus program to upload consciousness
  into cyberspace; the US military withdrew funding after three years of little progress.

## Factions in the campaign
| Faction | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Project | Player | Rebuilding with pre-war tech |
| Scavengers | Early enemy | Weak at first; ambush bunkers and hit-and-run harassment can hurt the unprepared |
| New Paradigm | Alpha enemy | More advanced than you; secretly Nexus-controlled |
| The Collective | Beta enemy | Industrial, urban; Nexus-controlled |
| Nexus | Final antagonist | Infects/takes over units and structures; strong elite army; VTOLs (build SAM sites) |

## Structure and mission flow
- Three phases: **Alpha, Beta, Gamma** (plus Gamma "end" content in some listings).
- **Persistent bases**: base and units carry over between missions in a campaign.
- **Timers**: most missions have a time limit to stop you building an unstoppable army.
- **Away missions**: you load up to 10 units onto a **transporter** and fly to a distant area of the sector.
- **Artifacts** gate research (see gameplay doc).
- Mission lengths and counts are reported inconsistently: "20 timed missions" vs. "50 missions". Probably they
  count different things (see gaps doc).

### Gamma mission list (from a Russian-language walkthrough, translated)
1. Establish a forward base
2. Destroy missile site
3. Assist Team Gamma (Nexus takes over Gamma's base and uses it against you)
4. Rescue Team Alpha
5. Defend your base (Nexus starts taking your base over, one structure at a time)
6. Rescue the scout team
7. The missile silo
8. Missile codes (described as the longest, slowest level)
9. Destroy Nexus main base

Alpha missions mentioned include raiders, a power-module artifact, a forward base and a counterattack. A full
Alpha and Beta list was not obtained.

## Campaign variants in modern builds
- **Remastered** campaign: expanding persistent bases and away missions, ongoing balance updates.
- **Campaign Selector** (4.5+) with **campaign balance mods**, including a built-in "Classic" balance
  and, in 4.7, the "Pumpkin v1.10" balance mod.
- Addon campaigns: **Reclamation** (4.5+), **Fractured Kingdom**.
- Difficulty: "Super Easy" added in 4.3; an "Insane+" tweak in 4.6.

## Design principle
Campaign pacing is built from *constraints*: the timer stops turtling, artifacts meter tech, transporters
limit what you bring. The story twist (all enemies are one AI) retroactively explains why the enemies feel
different but share infrastructure.
