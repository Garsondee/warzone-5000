|---|---|---|

| 4.7 | 6 Apr 2026 | "Legacy & Lobby Update": new netlobby (IPv4/IPv6, join by GameId, HTTPS), `--lobbyjoin` / `--lobbyspectate`, `emitSound()`, Pumpkin v1.10 balance mod, Vulkan fixes, more Remastered balance. |
