- VALIDATION (observation from the Workshop board, not explained from here): on the Hauler the 0-48 km/h time falls smoothly with engine power (`w5k viewer design hauler_4x4 --out DIR --lever engine_peak_power=F --skin off`; x1.1 -6.3%, x1.2 -10.9%, x1.3 -15.0%, x1.4 -18.1%, x1.5 -20.4%, x2.0 -24.8%), but the **gradient and step-height benchmarks jump** (gradient +14.3%, 0, +6.4%, -1.6%, -4.8% at x1.5, 0; step height +7.7%, 0, +7.7%, 0, +7.7%, +7.7%) and braking distance, which power should hardly touch, is +0.03%, +0.13%, +0.22%, +0.33%, **+4.0%** at x1.5, +3.1% at x2.0. The gradient search brackets to 0.01 (`bracket_hi - bracket_lo`), so a 5-point jump is not its resolution: either pass/fail is not monotone in slope for this launch, or the climb depends on the launch gear and clutch chaotically. Until then a small change on the board for a searched benchmark may be the benchmark's noise; a `resolution` (or the bracket) in `ProvingResult` would let the board say "inside the resolution" instead of "worse". The force ledgers of the x1.4 and x1.5 runs would show which term moved.
- GEOMETRY, FORGE (via ARCH): **the two carriers wind their loops opposite ways.** GEOMETRY's carrier has a rear sprocket and lists its wheels counter-clockwise in (z, y) with `direction` +1; FORGE's `carrier_tracked` (#164) has a front sprocket and lists them clockwise with `direction` -1. GEOMETRY's `Belt::round` / `link_frames` refuse FORGE's loop ("wheel 5 does not touch the belt": the wrong wheel named, because the whole loop winds the wrong way), and accept it reversed or mirrored (9.695 m, 65 links of 0.149 m).
