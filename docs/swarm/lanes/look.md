# Lane LOOK: realistic paint, weathering and light, specified once and shown the same in the browser and in Godot

## Mission
When you have succeeded, the HMMWV stands in the reference viewer in a believable three-tone NATO scheme: paint chipped at its edges, dirt in its creases, dust on its upward faces, a splash line up from the ground, and a dust trail behind its wheels on dry ground. Six schemes exist as RON parameter sets (NATO three-tone, woodland, three-colour desert, flecktarn, a Soviet-green scheme, a digital pixel pattern; three by M1), procedural and triplanar, no UVs, no toy colours. You specify each algorithm once (pseudocode in `docs/art/`, a reference implementation, numeric tests) and implement it twice: GLSL chunks for the three.js viewer (VIEWER hosts them through a documented hook) and Godot `.gdshader` files (GODOT integrates them). You also write `docs/art/ART-DIRECTION.md`. The owner will see the HMMWV in three schemes, clean and weathered, and a dust-trail strip.

## Acceptance tests (named; colour and light sentences)
- L1 `camo_pattern_is_deterministic_same_input_same_colour_bits`: 100k seeded points hash identically across runs and equal the golden on Linux and Windows; the reference uses only + - x / floor and integer ops (no libm), so the bits are portable and L7 can check the shaders against it.
- L2 `palette_coverage_matches_the_spec_within_3_percent`: each scheme on a flat tile (at least 1000 blob areas) and a large sphere (every orientation, blend zones included): each colour's fraction within 0.03 of spec. On one hull report the z-score, sqrt(p (1 - p) / n_eff), do not assert 3%.
- L3 `pattern_does_not_swim_when_the_object_moves_or_a_wheel_turns`: colour depends on node-local position and seed only; image level: a flat grid under two model matrices with the camera following gives identical pixels (world anchoring would not).
- L4 `pattern_is_stable_under_mesh_lod`: between two tessellations of the block and the cylinder, delta E (CIE76) under 3 at 99% of matched surface points.
- L5 `weathering_inputs_act_monotonically`: wear rises with `edge`, dirt with `cavity`, splash falls with height above ground, dust rises with the upward normal; zero flags give a clean flat face; triplanar weights sum to 1 and split 0.5 / 0.5 at a 45 degree normal.
- L6 `palette_colours_are_desaturated_and_inside_the_real_paint_range`: CIELAB chroma and linear albedo inside the bounds in `ART-DIRECTION.md` spanned by the cited schemes' own chips; every colour has a source; and `every_slot_kind_in_the_contract_has_a_material` (the variants parsed from `render.rs` equal the keys of `slots.ron`).
- L7 `shader_albedo_matches_the_reference_within_2_of_255_and_both_engines_agree_on_mid_grey`: 4096 points, albedo-only pass, headless Chromium (and Godot if S8 allows); an 18% grey card under the lighting rig within 6/255 across engines.
- L8 `visual_regression_against_baselines_within_perceptual_tolerance`: tier 1, CPU-reference swatches compared exactly; tier 2, viewer and Godot screenshots with mean delta E under 2 and no 16 x 16 block over 6. PNGs under 400 KB; re-blessed only with `W5K_BLESS=1` and `Golden-Change:`.
- L9 `dust_cloud_is_a_pure_function_of_replay_time`: seek forward, back, forward gives identical particles; a drag-free particle sits at p0 + v0 t + g t^2 / 2 (1e-9).
- L10 `dust_emission_rises_with_slip_and_speed_and_is_zero_on_asphalt_airborne_or_stopped`.

## Theory to explain in docs/theory/look.md
Triplanar projection is box mapping: three planar projections blended by the normal, so no UVs; noise thresholding is Photoshop's Clouds plus Threshold, and equalising the histogram makes the thresholds the coverage; anchoring in object space is Blender's Object texture coordinates, not world or window; `edge` and `cavity` masks are curvature and AO bakes driving wear and dirt; albedo is linear light, sRGB only at the edge, and real paint reflects little; tone mapping is a film curve from scene-referred to display-referred; a stateless particle is a point cache evaluated by formula, so scrubbing works.
