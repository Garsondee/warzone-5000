# Lane AI: a driver and gunner that sees only what it could see, knows its own vehicle and plays fair

## Mission
When you have succeeded, a vehicle with nobody at the controls drives the rough course to its objective and fights, fairly. It reads the world only through sensors with a range, a line of sight and a memory; it knows what its own vehicle can do (its capability table) and plans routes and speeds inside those limits; it notices when it is stuck and gets out, corrects its estimates when the world disagrees with its table, distrusts the table when damage changes the rig, leads a moving target and fires only when the gun is on. Its only output is `Command`, exactly what a human would emit, from an `Observation` built by a perception model, never ground truth. "Adapts" means tactics and measured capability, not machine learning. Squad: behaviour. Rank 3: you launch after contract-v0.2, which settles `Command` and `CapabilityTable`; the contract freezes at M1, so file CCRs early.

## Acceptance tests (named; physics sentences)
Oracle in brackets.
- 1, kernels: `astar_cost_equals_dijkstra_cost_on_random_grids` (200 seeded grids; hand-solved mazes); `planner_never_routes_through_no_go_soil_or_above_the_grade_limit` (500 seeded fields; no route returns `NoRoute`); `planned_curvature_never_exceeds_one_over_the_minimum_turn_radius`; `speed_profile_never_exceeds_sqrt_ay_over_curvature` (`max_lateral_accel_m_s2`); `speed_profile_never_exceeds_what_the_braking_distance_allows` (`braking_distance_m(v)` <= distance to the next slower point; v = sqrt(2 a d) for constant a); `pure_pursuit_on_a_line_has_damping_ratio_one_over_root_two` (small-angle kinematics: e'' + (2v/Ld) e' + (2v^2/Ld^2) e = 0, so overshoot e^-pi = 4.3% for any v and Ld, within 20%); `pure_pursuit_holds_a_circle_with_zero_steady_state_error` (kinematic: curvature is exactly 1/r at r = R; 0.2 m on the proxy after the entry transient).
- 2, driver: `completion_is_at_least_90_percent_over_20_seeds_on_the_rough_field`; `completion_survives_capability_limits_off_by_20_percent`; `stuck_recovery_escapes_a_constructed_trap_within_N_seconds` (U-shaped dead end with room to reverse; propose N = 20 s; all 20 seeds); `crawling_uphill_is_not_stuck` (negative control).
- 3, perception and fairness: `target_is_detected_exactly_when_in_range_in_line_of_sight_and_above_the_angular_size_threshold` (size / range >= theta_min); `mind_cannot_name_ground_truth_types` (source scan of `src/mind/**` for `WorldQuery`, `VehicleFrame`, `VehicleModel`, `w5k_sim`, `w5k_vehicle`, plus a `compile_fail` doctest that `Observation` cannot be built outside `perception`); `indistinguishable_worlds_give_identical_commands` (worlds differing only beyond sensor reach, such as an enemy behind a ridge or unseen soil and props: command-stream hash identical until a sensor can tell); `same_seed_same_command_stream` (golden on both platforms).
- 4, situation and adaptation: `cells_in_a_ridge_shadow_are_covered_where_the_wedge_formula_says` (shadow length = h / tan(elevation angle)); `capability_estimate_converges_to_the_true_limit_after_repeated_incidents` (`TrapVehicle`: within 10%, never more than 5% over); `a_changed_rig_hash_makes_the_ai_plan_conservatively_until_a_fresh_table_arrives`.
- 5, gunner: `lead_solution_matches_the_closed_form_intercept_time` (|r + v_t t| = v_s t, 1e-9 without drag); `fire_is_withheld_until_the_gun_error_is_inside_the_hit_tolerance_and_the_target_is_perceived` (negative control).
- 6, tactician: `posture_changes_at_most_once_per_hysteresis_window_in_a_steady_scene`.
- 7, M4 on real vehicles: `ai_parameters_are_identical_for_every_vehicle` (Param-set hash); `five_vehicles_complete_at_least_90_percent_over_100_seeds`; `unresolved_stuck_runs_are_under_3_percent` and `rollover_runs_are_under_1_percent` (from `Event::Stuck`, `Event::Rollover`).

## Theory to explain in `docs/theory/ai.md`
A costmap is a weight texture (obstacles dilated by the hull radius, the same morphological dilate as growing a mask); A* is Dijkstra with a heuristic, a best-first flood fill (a game engine's navmesh pathfinding, on a grid); pure pursuit is a look-at constraint chasing a carrot on a rail, with a closed loop of damping 0.707; the speed profile is a minimum over per-point limits plus a backward braking pass (limiting a dolly's speed on a spline); line of sight is a shadow ray; memory is temporal accumulation with decay (TAA history); utility AI scores options and picks the best with hysteresis (a Schmitt trigger); fairness means the AI plays with the player's controller and information, so its results mean something and the owner can take over any vehicle.

## Non-goals
Machine learning; cheating of any kind (ground truth, a hidden map, perfect reactions, peeking at the ledger or `LimitingFactor`); multiplayer; anything in the physics (no AI-only assists or forces); formations, radio and squad tactics (one vehicle and its own contacts); per-vehicle tuning; difficulty levels; rendering and UI.
