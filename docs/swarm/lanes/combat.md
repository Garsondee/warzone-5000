# Lane COMBAT: a gun that kicks, a shell that flies, armour that stops it, damage that slows the tank

## Mission
When you have succeeded, a tank slews its turret and the hull rocks the other way; the gun fires, the barrel slides back and the hull takes the rest of the impulse, momentum conserved to 1e-6 (physics, not animation); the shell flies a drag-bent arc and is stopped, glances off or goes through; and what it hits (engine, tracks, turret ring, gun, ammunition, crew) changes how the vehicle behaves: a thrown track and the tank circles. Every shot returns a `ShotReport` explaining its outcome: the combat force ledger. Squad: behaviour. Rank 2: run spike S6 first; steps 1 to 3 need no vehicle dynamics and proceed alongside the settling round.

## Theory to explain in `docs/theory/combat.md`
Recoil is Newton's third law: shell and gun are two masses pushed apart (a keyframed kick is a guess; this law sets its size), and it spins a hull when the turret slews (the swivel-chair astronaut). A PD servo is a spring-damper (the smooth-damp on cameras); a stabiliser is a gimbal. Ballistics is a particle system with drag. Thickness along a ray is an integral of an indicator, like transmittance in volume rendering, counted with polygon filling's even-odd rule; penetration is a threshold on it; ricochet is Fresnel-like. Damage is a health slider on a rig parameter.
