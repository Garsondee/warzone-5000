# Lane GEOMETRY: realistic hulls, wheels and tracks from lofted sections, closed, tagged by node and measured

## Mission
When you have succeeded, the M998 HMMWV and the M113 are recognisable machines in correct proportion (sloped lofted hulls with bevelled edges, greenhouse, bumpers, treaded wheels; road wheels, sprocket, idler, track run), every part a closed, outward-facing mesh tagged with the articulation node that moves it. The owner will see contact sheets, a lines plan (sections, sheer and half-breadth, as on a ship's drawing), the `edge` and `cavity` masks in false colour and a dimension table against the dossier, and can open the GLB in Blender. You also own the exact mass properties of a closed mesh (volume, centroid, inertia): one geometry feeds look, mass, armour and collision.

## Acceptance tests (named; geometry and mass sentences)
- A1 `rectangle_and_frustum_lofts_are_closed_boxes_with_the_prismatoid_volume`: V = w h L; two sections L (2 w1 h1 + 2 w2 h2 + w1 h2 + w2 h1) / 6; 1e-12; a cut at a station returns the input section.
- A2 `cylinder_volume_is_the_exact_n_gon_prism_and_within_0p1_percent_of_pi_r2_l_at_128_segments`: V = (N/2) R^2 sin(2 pi / N) L.
- A3 `box_and_cylinder_inertia_match_closed_forms_and_two_offset_boxes_obey_the_parallel_axis_theorem`: m (b^2 + c^2) / 12; prism m R^2 (1 + 2 cos^2(pi/N)) / 6; 1e-12.
- A4 `every_generated_part_is_closed_manifold_and_outward_facing`: both vehicles plus 200 seeded random in-range parameter sets; after welding at 1e-5 m every edge is shared by exactly two triangles traversed in opposite directions, signed volume > 0, no triangle under 1e-9 m^2; a mirrored part keeps its volume and negates centroid x; a ray crosses an even number of faces and a box chord is the wall-to-wall distance (armour thickness will rely on it).
- A5 `generation_is_deterministic_same_params_same_bytes`: f64 inside, f32 once at the end; a hash of every buffer's bits equals the golden in `crates/w5k_geo/tests/golden/` on Linux and Windows.
- A6 `edge_is_zero_on_flat_faces_and_cylinder_walls_and_follows_the_ramp_at_convex_edges`: box edge 1; each face of a 45 degree chamfer 0.5; concave crease 0; a 128-gon wall 0.
- A7 `cavity_is_zero_on_an_open_plate_and_matches_the_circular_segment_at_the_foot_of_a_wall`: blocked fraction (arccos a - a sqrt(1 - a^2)) / pi, a = d / L for distance d and ray cap L (cosine-weighted rays are uniform on the unit disc seen from above; a tall wall blocks the segment beyond a); 0.5 at the crease; 0.02 absolute at 1024 rays.
- A8 `wheel_outline_sag_is_below_1_mm_and_mesh_radius_equals_the_rig_radius_within_1_mm`: sag R (1 - cos(pi/N)); wheel bottoms touch the rig's ground plane within 1 mm.
- A9 `node_tags_cover_the_render_rig_joint_layout`: every station and articulation joint has parts on the right `NodeRole`; none on a missing node.
- A10 `hmmwv_and_m113_dimensions_match_the_dossier_within_3_percent`: length, width, height, wheelbase, track (or track width and contact length), clearance, wheel diameter, within ADR-0007's static class and the dossier band; wheelbase and track read from the rig; say which definition matched (over mirrors? with the top?); triangles inside the budget.
- A11 `track_links_tile_the_belt_with_no_gap_or_overlap`: belt length = perimeter of the taut wrap of the wheel circles (two equal circles 2 pi r + 2 c; the M113 layout by numerical integration, 1e-6); n = round(L / p), effective pitch within 1% of design; link k at (k p' + phase) mod L, phase = sprocket pitch radius x spin.

## Theory to explain in docs/theory/geometry.md
A loft is a ship's lines plan: station sections joined by surfaces (skinning a curve network in Maya). The divergence theorem turns a volume integral into a sum of signed tetrahedra (the 3D-print toolbox reports volume this way); centroid and inertia are first and second moments of mass, the inertia tensor being the covariance of the mass distribution (what a PCA oriented-box fit computes for points). Why watertight and outward matter: volume sign, ray parity for armour thickness. `edge` and `cavity` are curvature and AO bakes (Substance Painter) held per vertex, so no UVs.
