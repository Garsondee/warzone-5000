# Tracked steering units: what DRIVE gives TRACKS' skid model

A short note (no code change). DRIVE's four steering units are merged (`crates/w5k_drive/src/driveline.rs`, tests in `powertrain.rs`); this says what each one does at the sprockets so the skid model can be built against them. The demand is `DriveInputs::steer` (+1 = full right: the right track is the slow one); DRIVE returns one torque per sprocket and reads each sprocket's speed, inertia and reaction from `ShaftState`.

| Unit | What DRIVE does | Turn it produces | Where the power goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Controlled differential** (brake steering) | An open differential gives both sprockets equal drive torque; the steering brake on the inside sprocket takes torque off that side. | Radius is not fixed: it is whatever the brake torque and the ground's turning resistance settle at (brake torque up to the brake's `max_torque_nm`). | Dissipated as heat in the inner brake (visible in `brake_temps_k`). |
| **Clutch-brake** | Both sprockets are locked to one speed straight ahead. The first half of the stick slips the inner clutch open (its drive torque falls to zero and the outer side carries all of it), the second half also brakes the inner sprocket. | Half stick: the inner track coasts, so the tank drifts wide on the ground's resistance alone. Full stick: the inner track is braked, so it pivots about it (radius about half the gauge). | Dissipated in the inner clutch and brake. |
| **Double differential** (regenerative) | An implicit servo holds `(w_left - w_right) = d s (w_left + w_right)`: a fixed *ratio* of track speeds, `d` per gear (`diff_ratio_by_gear`, default 0.35). | Fixed radius per gear: with `d` = (v_o - v_i)/(v_o + v_i), `R = B / (2 d)` for track gauge `B` (about 1.4 B at the default). Detents quantise the stick. | Nothing is wasted: the unit moves power from the inner to the outer sprocket. |
| **Hydrostatic** | The same servo with a fixed speed *difference* `diff_speed_rad_s x s` whatever the speed. With `works_in_neutral` it pivots with the gearbox in neutral. | Radius grows with speed: `R = B v / (r_sprocket diff_speed)`; a pivot in neutral. | Through the pump and motor (the neutral pivot does not yet draw its power from the engine: PROVISIONAL(steer-neutral-power)). |

## What the skid model must supply, and what to expect back
- **The kinematic units (double differential, hydrostatic) impose the track speeds**, so TRACKS only needs to get the *forces* right: the servo's torque pair (clamped to `max_steer_torque_nm`) appears as the opposite sprocket torques. Whether the unit can turn the vehicle is then decided by the ground: the pivot moment the ground resists is about `mu W L / 4` (the S3 criterion), the track force difference needed is `2 M / B`, and the sprocket torque pair is `(2 M / B) r_sprocket / 2` per side. If that exceeds `max_steer_torque_nm`, the servo saturates and the turn radius opens up: the model should show it, not hide it.
- **The braking units (controlled differential, clutch-brake) do not fix a ratio**, so the radius must *emerge* from your lateral-resistance law: the inner sprocket's reaction torque and speed feed back through `ShaftState`, and DRIVE's brake is solved as "the torque that holds or slows the shaft, clamped to capacity", so a stopped inner track stays stopped and cannot be pushed backwards by the brake.
- **Straight-line running** needs the sprocket speeds to stay equal under unequal ground resistance: the clutch-brake locks them, the double differential and hydrostatic servo them, the controlled differential only keeps equal *torque* (it is an open differential), so a track on softer ground will run faster and the vehicle will pull to that side: that is the real behaviour and a good test of the skid model.
- **Interface wishes (none needed now):** sprocket speeds and reaction torques every substep (already in `ShaftState`), the loaded sprocket pitch radius (so the servo target can be stated in vehicle speed), and later a crawl hook (a flag asking DRIVE to hold the engine near its torque peak and the first gear) when TRACKS asks.

Tests that pin the above: `steering_unit_demand_turns_the_two_outputs_in_opposite_senses`, `a_hydrostatic_unit_holds_a_speed_difference_whatever_the_speed_and_pivots_in_neutral`, `a_double_differential_gives_the_stated_fractional_speed_difference`, `detents_snap_the_demand_and_the_dead_band_goes_straight`, `brake_steering_units_heat_the_inner_brake_only` (all in `crates/w5k_drive/src/powertrain.rs`).
