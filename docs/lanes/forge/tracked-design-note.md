# FORGE design note: the tracked compile (slice 2, stage B)

Contract pin: `contract-v0.3` = integration `0982843`. Read first: `docs/lanes/tracks/design-note.md` (what the track solver reads), `docs/swarm/requests/geometry-forge-tracks.md` (what the skin reads), `docs/architecture/redteam/tracked.md` (the gaps already settled in the rig), `box_tank()` (the reference layout). Target vehicle: a fictional **carrier** of the M113 class (about 11 t, 5 road wheels a side, aluminium hull, no turret), id `carrier_tracked`; the dossier link stays in VALIDATION's mapping. Every number below that the def cannot state yet is a PROVISIONAL(CCR-forge) extra, exactly as for the wheeled compile; the `T` items of `forge-ccr-vehicledef.md` (rollers, idler, bogies, belt thickness) are still not in `def.rs` in 0.3.

## What the compile produces
`compile_tracked(&VehicleDef, &Extras) -> Result<Compiled, Vec<Rejection>>` (the same `Compiled`, `Rejection` and report as the wheeled path; `compile()` dispatches on `RunningGearDef`). Output: `stations` for both sides, one `TrackDef` per side, `drivetrain` with a `SteerUnit` over the two sprockets, brakes on the sprockets, a Hull proxy and a Belly proxy, and the `RenderRig` joints.

## 1. Stations and loop order (per side; left side first, then right)
Loop order from the sprocket (the contract's rule, validated): **sprocket, return rollers (sprocket end to idler end, along the top run), idler, road wheels (idler end to sprocket end, along the ground run)**. `stations` of the rig and `TrackDef.stations` use that order, so a side's stations are contiguous and the joint names follow it (`l_spr`, `l_ret0..`, `l_idl`, `l_r1..`; GEOMETRY reads the order from the rig).

| kind | radius | travel | mass | suspension |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sprocket | **pitch** radius `pitch / (2 sin(pi / teeth))` (def `sprocket_diameter_m` is the pitch diameter; the compile checks the two agree within 1% when the teeth count is given) | none | 0 (in the hull) | rigid |
| idler | `idler_diameter_m / 2` | horizontal tensioner (`bump_dir` along the hull), small | 0 | rigid, tension from the belt (`tension_n`) |
| return roller | extras | none | 0 | rigid |
| road wheel | `road_wheel_diameter_m / 2` | vertical, `bump_travel_m` and `droop_travel_m` | unsprung per wheel (extras) | spring from the ride-frequency slider |

Positions. Hull frame: origin at the hull box centre, +Z back (D3). Road wheels are evenly spaced; `ground_contact_length_m = (n - 1) s + d_rw` (first to last centre plus one wheel diameter), which fixes the spacing `s` and the wheel centres about the contact centre (shifted so the COM sits where the statics need it only for the loads, not for the geometry). Sprocket and idler positions come from `sprocket_from_front_m` and `idler_from_front_m` (extras, hull-length scale), the roller x positions by the rule below. Lateral: `track_gauge_m / 2` each side. Height: a road wheel centre is `r + thickness` above the ground, i.e. `rest.y = r_rw + t - ride_height` (validated: wheel bottom plus belt on the ground plane); `ride_height = clearance + height / 2` as in the wheeled path (the hull box is measured as GEOMETRY's request proposes: width over the tracks, clearance belly to ground).
**Rollers** sit with their tops on the straight line from the idler's top to the sprocket's top, belt thickness allowed for (GEOMETRY's rule, so the belt touches them), at even spacing, count from extras.
The idler's and sprocket's heights are their own numbers (extras; defaults from the box tank layout scaled): the top run is then a straight line between their tops.

## 2. Masses and loads
`hull.mass_kg` includes the top run of each belt and every rigid station (the contract's convention: their `unsprung_mass_kg` is 0). Road-wheel unsprung mass per wheel is an extra; `total_mass_kg` adds the ground run `mass_per_m * contact_length` automatically. Sprung weight is shared over the road wheels by the same statics as the wheeled compile (linear in position, `axle_loads` generalised from axles to road-wheel rows); preloads sum to the sprung weight (validated at 5%, the compile hits it exactly). Hull COM and inertia: the same box-plus-parallel-axis kernel, now with the hull mass breakdown still one lump (the mass-item kernel is later).

## 3. Springs and dampers
Ride frequency per road wheel: `k = m_corner (2 pi f)^2` as in the wheeled compile, but **there is no tyre in series** (the belt is massless and its stiffness is `TrackDef.wheel_contact`, N/m, which is in series with the soil in the solver, not with the spring), so the spring is the ride rate (decision D1 does not apply; stated in the report). `damping_ratio` gives the mean damper `2 zeta sqrt(k m)` split by `rebound_to_bump`. The spring kind in the rig is `Linear` for every `SuspensionKind` first (torsion and volute are reported as approximated, as with the leaf in the wheeled compile); a `Torsion` mapping (rate per radian from the arm length and rest angle) is the next refinement once TRACKS' bench needs it. Bump stops from the extras (rate high enough to show on a drop test; progression as wheeled).

## 4. The track
Per side one `TrackDef`: `width_m`, `pitch_m`, `contact_length_m` from the def; `mass_per_m_kg = track_mass_per_side / belt_length`; `belt_length_m` computed from the loop (tangent segments plus arcs round the wheel circles with the path radius = tip + thickness / 2; the sprocket's pitch radius): the closed-loop perimeter, checked `>= contact_length` (validated); `thickness_m`, `sprocket_teeth`, `samples`, `tension_n`, `resist_c0`, `wheel_contact`, `shoe_mu_scale` and `shoe_mu_scale_soft` from the def and extras. GEOMETRY and TRACKS check the same numbers back (a GEOMETRY test fails if the drawn ground run differs from `contact_length_m` by more than 5%).

## 5. Drivetrain, steering and brakes
Engine, coupling and gearbox as wheeled. Driveline: `SteerUnit { kind, ratio, law, children: [Output(left), Output(right)] }` from `SteeringUnitSliders` (kind and ratio are in the def); the `SteerLaw` fields (`diff_ratio_by_gear`, `detents`, `diff_speed_rad_s`, `works_in_neutral`, `max_steer_torque_nm`, `steer_brakes`) are extras (T2). Outputs at the two sprockets with `final_drive_ratio` from the def. Brakes on the sprockets (the contract: tracked vehicles brake their sprockets), `BrakeLocation::BeforeFinalDrive` or at station per extras, torque authored as an axle-pair spec (the same `axle_torque_nm` path as the wheeled trucks, one entry for the pair of sprockets); parking on. Skid steering in kid mode is TRACKS and ARCH's glue; FORGE only supplies the unit and the law.

## 6. Collision proxies and the belly
Hull proxy: the hull box. Belly proxy (`ProxyRole::Belly`): a box of the hull's width between the tracks (`track_gauge - track_width`) and `belly_length_m` (extras), its underside exactly `ground_clearance_m` above the ground: the shape TRACKS' belly-drag law needs (their `BellyDef` request is satisfied by this proxy until the contract carries a `BellyDef`). This also gives ground clearance a real effect in the tracked rig.

## 7. Substeps and rejection rules
Substeps by the contract rule from the stiffest mode: road-wheel hop on `wheel_contact` in parallel with the spring and the engaged bump stop (`sqrt((k_s + k_wc + k_stop) / m_u)`), plus the hull heave on the series rates; rejected above 8 with the mode named (the red-team's data: 4 to 7 for a tank). Rejections, each with its reason: fewer than 2 road wheels, spacing smaller than a wheel diameter (wheels overlap), a belt shorter than the contact length, a sprocket pitch radius that disagrees with the teeth count, a COM outside the road-wheel span, `k_ride >= wheel_contact` (the belt contact would be the softer spring), any `Param` outside its band.

## 8. Extras added (all `Param`, sidecar `<id>.extras.ron`, `#[serde(default)]` so the wheeled sidecars are unchanged)
`TrackedExtras`: `sprocket_from_front_m`, `idler_from_front_m`, `sprocket_height_m`, `idler_height_m`, `idler_diameter_m`, `return_rollers` (count) and `roller_diameter_m`, `belt_thickness_m`, `sprocket_teeth`, `samples`, `tension_n`, `resist_c0`, `resist_c1_s_m`, `road_wheel_unsprung_kg`, `road_wheel_inertia_kg_m2`, `wheel_contact_stiffness_n_m`, `wheel_contact_damping_ns_m`, `tensioner_travel_m`, `belly_length_m`, `steer_law` (T2). The CCR text for moving them into `def.rs` stays in `forge-ccr-vehicledef.md` section T.

## 10. Risks
`TrackedDef` in 0.3 cannot say rollers, idler, bogies or belt thickness: extras carry them until the T items reach `def.rs`. Torsion and volute springs are approximated by a linear rate in T1. The M113-class numbers are archetype estimates; none from memory as a dossier figure. TRACKS' `GearConfig::from_track_def` reads `wheel_x_m` and the sprocket radius from the compiled rig: T1's station layout is the contract between us (TRACKS: please read the table in section 1).
