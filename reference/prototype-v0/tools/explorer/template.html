What the sample is made of

Every dot above is a vehicle like these: the same catalogue of parts, mixed by the same physics.

Evaluation is the quick path (exact-volume mass, four-direction armour): within roughly 10 to 20 percent of the full build. The sample is stratified, so every hull-and-gear mix gets the same number of attempts at random sizes, weapons and masts; it shows the shape of the space, not the odds of a random roll.

Every constant behind the numbers (tyre pressure limit, melt energy, rotor stall, anti-gravity cost, leg safety factors) is a hypothesis chosen to give a believable shape. Reproduce a design with the command in the inspector; regenerate the sample with w5k space content --out out.
